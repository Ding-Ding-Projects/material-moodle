    See it running

    Real screenshots from a Material Moodle site, on a laptop and on a phone.

    Prefer the terminal?

    One command on any Linux server. A menu asks a few questions, generates the passwords and settings, starts Moodle and prints your link.

    bash <(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ding-Ding-Projects/material-moodle/main/material/install.sh)

    Try it in your browser

    The demo runs the sign-in page, the command palette with its regex builder, a celebration and an error screen, all without a server.
