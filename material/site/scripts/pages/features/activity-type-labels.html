Activity type names and personal titles

Show one assignment as a "Dropbox", add Post a new Dropbox buttons, and give people titles that keep their role.

Availablelocal_materialtools

Type names for one activity

Every activity's settings form has an optional Type name field. One assignment can appear as a Dropbox, another as a Lab report, while both stay ordinary assignments with the same permissions, grading and reports. Leave the field empty to bring back the standard name.

Quick post buttons

In editing mode, teachers see buttons such as Post a new Dropbox at the top of the course. Each creates an activity of the right type and gives it the type name in one step.

The buttons come from the Quick post buttons setting, one Name|activity per line:

Dropbox|assign
Reading check|quiz

Personal titles

Give one teacher or student their own title, such as "Coach" or "Intern", shown instead of their role name, for the whole site or for one course. A course title wins over a site title. Their role and permissions stay exactly the same.

Type names and titles are display names only: they never change roles, capabilities or activity types.
