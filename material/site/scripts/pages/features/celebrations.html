Celebrations

Success messages burst with confetti; errors shake and flash so they are not missed.

Availabletheme_material

Success and error

  • Success messages bounce and confetti in the palette colours bursts from them. At the Dramatic level 64 pieces fly and two rings expand; at Normal, 28 pieces and no rings.
  • Error messages shake. At the Dramatic level the window also flashes red briefly.

Each message is celebrated once. Passkey sign-in, passkey registration and vocabulary uploads use the same effects.

Levels

Celebration level (theme_material | celebrationlevel) is Subtle, Normal or Dramatic (default). Subtle keeps the error shake and skips confetti. Celebrations never run when motion is calm or off, or when the device asks for reduced motion.

Accessibility

The effects are decoration only and hidden from assistive technology; the message itself stays an ordinary Moodle alert with its text and role.
