Forced password change and optional passkey

New students choose their own password, then may add a passkey. The passkey step can be skipped.

Availablelocal_materialtoolsauth_passkey

Three steps

  1. New passwordMoodle asks for a new password right after the first sign-in with the temporary one.
  2. Passkey (optional)"Faster sign-in next time?" with Add a passkey and Not now.
  3. DoneThe student continues to the page they wanted, or the dashboard.

The passkey step appears once. Adding a passkey anywhere, or choosing Not now, clears the offer. It never interrupts AJAX requests, "log in as" sessions or a pending password change.

Settings

Offer a passkey to new accounts (auth_passkey | offeronfirstlogin), on by default.

Limits

Only accounts created with Add a student get the passkey offer. Accounts from upload or self-registration do not.
