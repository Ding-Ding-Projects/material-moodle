Motion and reduced motion

Expressive, calm or off. Devices that ask for reduced motion always get the calm version.

Availabletheme_material

What moves

The theme animates with Material's emphasized easing: content rises in when a page opens, menus and dialogs scale in, buttons show a touch ripple where they are pressed, and the sign-in page has slowly morphing shapes in the palette colours.

LevelWhat moves
expressive (default)Everything: page rise, ripples, menu and dialog motion, sign-in shapes, confetti and rings.
calmShort fades only. Shapes and ripples stop and celebrations are skipped.
offNo animations or transitions at all.

The level is written to the <html> element as data-md-motion before the first paint, so CSS follows it without waiting for JavaScript.

Reduced motion

When the device asks for reduced motion, CSS cuts every animation and transition to almost nothing, and JavaScript does not start page transitions, ripples or celebrations, whatever the site setting says. Nothing that carries meaning depends on motion.

This website follows the same rules. Try the Motion setting in the display settings menu at the top of any page.

Settings

  • Motion (theme_material | motion): expressive, calm or off.
  • Page transitions (theme_material | pagetransitions): on by default.
  • Touch ripples (theme_material | ripples): on by default, used only when motion is expressive.

Motion is a site-wide setting; there is no per-person motion preference in Moodle yet.
