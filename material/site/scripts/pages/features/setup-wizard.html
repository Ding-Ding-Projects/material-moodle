First-run setup wizard

Colour, sign-in page, passkeys, vocabulary and wording in six guided steps, with Back, Next, Skip and resume.

Availablelocal_materialtools

What it does

After a fresh install with the Docker stack, the first administrator who signs in is taken once to Set up Material Moodle. It is also always available under Site administration › Users › Material tools.

The steps

  1. ColourPick a seed colour (or any colour) and the motion level. The whole palette changes at once.
  2. Sign-in pageWrite the heading and text people see on the coloured side of the sign-in page.
  3. PasskeysShow "Sign in with a passkey" and choose whether new accounts are offered a passkey.
  4. VocabularySee whether a vocabulary pack is loaded and open the vocabulary page.
  5. WordingRename Teacher, Student and Assignment for the whole site.
  6. DoneAdd your first student or go to the dashboard.

Moving around

  • Next saves the step; Back returns without losing anything.
  • Optional steps have Skip for now.
  • The wizard remembers the last step, so leaving and coming back resumes where you stopped.
  • Every choice stays editable later in the normal settings pages.

Permissions

The wizard needs moodle/site:config. The one-time redirect only happens for site administrators, never during AJAX or command-line requests or while logged in as another user.
