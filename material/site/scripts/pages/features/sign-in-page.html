Configurable sign-in page

Your own heading, text, labels and optional photo beside a large passkey button.

Availabletheme_material

Layout

The coloured side shows the site's short name, a large heading, a sentence or two of text and a row of short labels over slowly morphing shapes in the palette colours. The form side is restyled in Material and, when passkey sign-in is enabled, shows a large Sign in with a passkey button above the username field.

Settings

SettingDefault
Sign-in headingLearning, in full colour.
Sign-in textSign in with your fingerprint, face or security key, or with your username and password.
Sign-in labelsPasskeys, Dark mode, Material 3
Sign-in imageNone (JPG, PNG or WebP replaces the shapes)

All text goes through Moodle's multi-language filters. If Instructions are set in Moodle's authentication settings, they replace the heading and text.
