Passkeys

Sign in once with your face, fingerprint or security key. No password to remember, and nothing to phish.

Why passkeys

Cannot be phished

A passkey only works on the site it was made for, so a fake sign-in page gets nothing.

Nothing to type

The device offers the passkey. Students do not type a username or a password.

Passwords still work

Passkeys are added to existing accounts. The password stays as a fallback.

Turn it on

  1. Enable the pluginSite administration › Plugins › Authentication › Manage authentication › Passkeys. The Docker first run does this for you.
  2. Use a fixed addressPasskeys belong to one host name. Use your own domain or a named tunnel before people add them.
  3. People add passkeysFrom Preferences › Passkeys, or from the optional step after a new account's first sign-in.

The passkey manager

Everyone manages their own passkeys: add one with a name such as "My phone", see when each was last used, rename or remove it. Keep at least two passkeys on different devices.
